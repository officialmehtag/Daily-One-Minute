1. No refunds

All subscription payments — Pro and Platinum — are non-refundable. Once a payment is processed, we do not offer refunds for any reason, including but not limited to:

There is no mid-cycle cancellation with a refund. You may cancel your subscription at any time through the Manage Subscription option inside the tool, but your access will continue — and no refund will be issued — until the end of the billing period you have already paid for.

If you believe a charge was made in error or you notice an unexpected charge, contact us at hello@gauravmehta.me within 7 days of the charge and we will investigate. Initiating a chargeback without contacting us first may result in account suspension.

All payments are processed securely through Razorpay. We do not store your card details. For payment-related disputes, Razorpay's own policies may also apply.
