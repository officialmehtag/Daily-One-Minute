Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our tools. We keep it plain and honest. If you have questions, email us at hello@gauravmehta.me.

Operated by: Tale Breeze

Contact person: Gaurav Mehta

Email: hello@gauravmehta.me

1. What we collect

When you use our tools, we collect the following:

  • Email address — entered when you first open the tool. Used to identify your account and track your session count.
  • Session data — the number of practice sessions you complete, your first and last seen dates, and the country you are accessing from (detected automatically).
  • Self-evaluation responses — if you use the post-session self-evaluation feature (Platinum only), we store your answers to the three questions and the topic you spoke on.
  • Payment information — if you subscribe to Pro or Platinum, your subscription status, plan type, and expiry date are stored. We do not store card numbers or payment method details. All payment processing is handled by Razorpay.
  • Marketing consent — whether you opted in to receive occasional speaking tips and updates.

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not record, upload, or store your video recordings. All recordings are processed entirely in your browser and stay on your device unless you choose to download or share them.
  • We do not use your camera or microphone for any purpose other than the recording you initiate.
  • We do not sell your data to any third party.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

3. How we use your data

  • To identify your account and track your session progress
  • To verify your subscription status and unlock paid features
  • To send occasional speaking tips and updates if you opted in
  • To improve the tool based on aggregate usage patterns
  • To provide coaching context if you are a client of Gaurav Mehta

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in Supabase, a secure cloud database hosted on AWS infrastructure. Payment data is stored with Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. Both services comply with industry-standard security practices.

5. How long we keep your data

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you would like your data deleted, email us at hello@gauravmehta.me and we will remove it within 7 business days.

6. Third-party services

Our tools use the following third-party services:

  • Razorpay — payment processing. Their privacy policy applies to payment data.
  • Supabase — database and user data storage.
  • Vercel — hosting and server functions.
  • Anthropic Claude API — generates speaking frameworks, examples, and coaching tips for Platinum users. Topic and self-evaluation data is sent to generate responses. No personally identifiable information is sent.
  • ipapi.co — detects your country for analytics purposes. No personal data is shared.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw marketing consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@gauravmehta.me.

8. Cookies

Our tools use browser Local Storage to remember your email and session count between visits. This is not a cookie but serves a similar purpose. It stays on your device and is not shared with anyone. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

9. Children

Our tools are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it immediately.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. Continued use of the tool after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at hello@gauravmehta.me.
